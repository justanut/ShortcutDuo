# Shortcut Duo Privacy Notice

**Last updated: 28 October 2026**

Shortcut Duo is a Camera Control replacement for the iPhone. This notice explains what it does, and does not do, with your data.

## The short version

- Shortcut Duo **does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell** anything you type or do.
- Shortcut Duo **never connects to the internet**. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no accounts.
- Shortcut Duo requests for camera access for the sole process of enabling it to be selected as a replacement in settings.

## What is saved on your device

Nothing

## Information from Apple

If you download Shortcut Duo from the App Store or test it through TestFlight, Apple may collect usage and crash information under Apple's own privacy policy. We don't receive your personal information from Apple. If you choose to share analytics or send TestFlight feedback, that is handled by Apple and any feedback you write is seen by the developer.

## Children

Shortcut Duo doesn't collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this notice

If this notice changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
